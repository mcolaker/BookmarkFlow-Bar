BookmarkFlow reads your own Chrome Bookmark Bar. Bookmark content never leaves your browser.

Privacy choice

Choose whether to enable bookmark and page access

BookmarkFlow stays off until you agree. If enabled, it handles the following data only to provide its bookmark workspace:

  • Bookmark titles, URLs, folders, and favicons are read from Chrome and changed only after your actions.
  • On regular websites, the current URL, title, and limited layout context are processed locally for matching, visibility, and non-overlapping placement.
  • Display settings may use Chrome Sync; per-site, pinned-folder, and folder-color choices stay in local Chrome storage.
  • A new-tab search is sent by Chrome to your selected default search provider only after you submit it.

Maprins Games does not receive bookmark, page, preference, or search data. There are no analytics, ads, or data sales.

Browser bar

Hide Chrome’s native bookmarks bar

Ctrl + Shift + B

BookmarkFlow cannot change Chrome’s own interface. If the native bookmarks bar is visible, right-click it and turn off “Show bookmarks bar,” or use Ctrl + Shift + B.

In Brave, if the shortcut is not enough, open Settings > Appearance > Show bookmarks and choose Never. “Only on new tab page” keeps the native bar visible on new tabs.

This does not delete bookmarks. BookmarkFlow continues reading the same Bookmark Bar data.
