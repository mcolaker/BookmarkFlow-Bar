## 2. LinkedIn Announcement (Professional English)

```text
I am excited to share the release of BookmarkFlow Bar v0.3.1 — introducing our 5th official theme: Turquoise Glow! 🌊💎⚡

BookmarkFlow Bar turns your browser bookmarks into a customizable multi-row toolbar, a keyboard-driven Spotlight command palette, and an organized new-tab workspace — with 100% zero-cloud, offline-first privacy.

What's new in v0.3.1:

💎 Turquoise Glow Theme (BF-UX-024):
Our 5th official curated theme, engineered with high-contrast vivid turquoise accents (#22d3ee), deep oceanic dark backgrounds (#061318, #0a1a20), and crisp icy-turquoise text (#ecfeff). Available immediately in popup settings with complete EN/TR locale parity.

🌊 Turquoise Abyss New Tab Wallpaper & Folder Glow (BF-UX-025):
A new ambient deep-ocean radial gradient wallpaper option for your New Tab page, complemented by glowing turquoise folder icons (.bf-folder-icon, .nt-folder-icon) and deep ocean folder cards.

✨ Edge Peek Strip & Search Action Accents (BF-UX-026):
The minimalist hidden-bar restore handle (.bf-edge-restore) now illuminates with a vivid turquoise vertical gradient upon approach. Direct link save chips (.bf-command-action-chip, .nt-search-action-chip, .nt-inline-save-btn) feature glowing turquoise borders and focus rings.

⚡ Toast Progress Bars & Focus Ring Refinements (BF-UX-027):
Micro toast progress indicators (.bf-toast-progress, .nt-toast-progress) now animate with a fluid turquoise gradient, while search inputs across all surfaces gain high-visibility turquoise focus rings for enhanced keyboard accessibility.

🎯 Shortcuts Grid Glow & Health Inspector Metrics (BF-UX-028):
Top shortcut cards on New Tab reflect radiant turquoise borders on hover and keyboard navigation. The Bookmark Maintenance Center synchronizes active theme to illuminate health check metric badges and filter chips.

⏰ Clock / Greeting Gradient & Folder Merge Physics (BF-UX-029):
Dynamic digital clock (#clockDisplay) and greeting greetings (#greetingDisplay) on New Tab feature modern icy-turquoise text clip gradients and drop shadows. The duplicate folder merger button (#merge.primary) receives a vivid turquoise neon gradient, button press physics, and turquoise :focus-visible styling.

🛡️ Quality & Compatibility:
- 104/104 automated tests and strict public-tree governance passing.
- Deterministic builds and packages for Chrome / Chromium MV3, Firefox, and Microsoft Edge.
- 100% local-first data integrity. Zero tracking. Zero cloud dependencies.

Chrome Web Store:
```
