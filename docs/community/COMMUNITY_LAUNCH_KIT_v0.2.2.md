## 2. LinkedIn Announcement (Professional English)

```text
I am thrilled to announce the release of BookmarkFlow Bar v0.2.2! 🚀⚡

Managing browser bookmarks has traditionally been a clunky, multi-step chore: navigating menus, choosing folders in endless trees, and switching contexts. With v0.2.2, we are introducing a transformative, zero-friction link capture and intent routing experience — without compromising our strict local-first privacy architecture:

What's new in v0.2.2:
⚡ Zero-Latency Intent Engine (BookmarkIntentRoutingEngine): As you type in the search bar or in-page Spotlight (Alt+Shift+K), your intent is classified locally in milliseconds across 6 modes (URL, Command, Tag, Folder, Tab Switch, Search) with live smart routing badges.
⭐ Instant Search-Card Folder Chips: Paste any web link into the search box, and instant action chips appear directly in the suggestion card: "Save to Bar" or "Save to Folder". Zero clicks required to open a dialog.
🔔 Micro Toast Feedback: Immediate confirmation via an elegant 1.8-second golden-bordered toast notification verifying the exact destination folder.
📁 Smart Folder Memory & Quick Chips: Remembers your last targeted folder across all surfaces and renders one-click chip selectors for your most frequent folders.
💻 Windows Desktop Companion: System-wide Win+Shift+B global shortcut and system tray controller, operating seamlessly even when the browser is minimized.
🎥 Agentic Motion & Media QA: Automated 60 FPS animation smoothness and visual hygiene verification powered by Playwright and Gemini Agentic Video engine.

Under the hood:
- Strict closed Shadow DOM encapsulation preserving host page styling and security.
- 86/86 automated unit, contract, and Chromium browser security regression tests passing with fail-closed validation.
- 100% client-side execution with zero analytics, zero telemetry, and Apache 2.0 open-source licensing.

Multi-browser distribution packages for Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge are available now.
```
