# BookmarkFlow Bar Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the BookmarkFlow Bar community welcoming, safe, and equitable for everyone who participates in good faith. We value people of every background, identity, experience level, ability, language, and perspective.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and professional respect;
- welcome different viewpoints and give constructive, actionable feedback;
- focus criticism on ideas and contributions rather than people;
- acknowledge mistakes, accept responsibility, and make a good-faith effort to repair harm;
- respect personal boundaries, confidentiality, attribution, and the privacy-first values of the project;
- keep issues, pull requests, reviews, and community discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in any project space:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, stalking, or sustained unwanted attention;
- discriminatory, demeaning, insulting, or sexualized language, imagery, or conduct;
- personal attacks, trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith escalation;
- publishing or soliciting another person's private information without explicit permission;
- impersonation, evasion of moderation, plagiarism, or failure to credit contributed work;
- spam, deceptive promotion, or other conduct that undermines a safe and productive community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, releases, and other spaces managed by BookmarkFlow Bar. It also applies when someone officially represents the project, including through an official account, address, event, or public communication.

## Reporting an issue

Report a possible Code of Conduct violation privately by emailing [contact@maprins.games](mailto:contact@maprins.games) with the subject **BookmarkFlow Bar Code of Conduct report**. Include relevant links, dates, and a concise description, but do not send passwords, authentication tokens, personal bookmark exports, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Do not open a public GitHub issue for a private conduct report. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as possible, and identifying information will be shared only when necessary to investigate or address the incident. Retaliation against a person who makes a good-faith report is itself a violation.

Security vulnerabilities are handled separately through [GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/09mc/BookmarkFlow-Bar/security/advisories/new), as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). If a conduct report concerns the primary project maintainer or the listed reporting channel is unsafe to use, contact [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) for platform-level abuse reporting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and consistently enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that violate this Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when doing so is safe and appropriate.

Reports will be evaluated according to their context, impact, severity, and pattern of behavior. Depending on those factors, maintainers may apply one or more of the following actions:

1. **Correction or warning:** A private explanation of the violation and the behavior expected going forward.
2. **Temporary limitation:** A time-limited restriction on participation, communication, or interaction with specific community members.
3. **Temporary suspension:** Removal from project spaces for a defined period, with documented conditions for return.
4. **Permanent ban:** Permanent removal from project spaces when serious harm or repeated violations make continued participation unsafe.

Lower steps may be skipped when necessary to protect the community. Enforcement actions will normally be handled privately with the involved parties while prioritizing safety, fairness, and confidentiality.
