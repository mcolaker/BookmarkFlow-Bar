# Support

BookmarkFlow Bar is maintained in public on GitHub.

## Get help or share an idea

- Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/mcolaker/BookmarkFlow-Bar/discussions) for usage questions, workflow ideas, and open-ended proposals.
- Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/mcolaker/BookmarkFlow-Bar/issues) for reproducible bugs and focused feature requests.
- Check the [README](README.md), [roadmap](ROADMAP.md), and existing threads before opening a new report.

Support is provided on a best-effort basis. The maintainer aims to acknowledge well-scoped public reports within three business days, but this is not a service-level agreement.

## Security reports

Do not disclose a suspected vulnerability in a public issue or discussion. Follow the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use synthetic data in reproduction steps.

## Privacy

Never attach a real browser profile, personal bookmark export, browsing history, credential, token, or private URL to a support request. Redact unrelated information from screenshots and logs.
